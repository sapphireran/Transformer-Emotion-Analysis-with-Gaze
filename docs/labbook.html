Reader 3 compaction

MATLAB task-1 subject 2 drops sentences 150–249 and 399, then writes 3_SR.csv with compact ids 0–298. Compact 150 is original 250. The published sentence mean is a positional 0→NaN average, so nFixations moves on 246 sentences (target 246) and SentLen — which is not even a gaze feature — moves on 147 (target 147). Worst nFixations shift is id 239, |Δ|=0.211.

Word streams stay aligned for the first 2594 tokens (sentences 0–149), then word_averages_v2.csv averages different words at the same row index.

On the clean overlap, nFixations mean pairwise r = 0.362 (range 0.101–0.602), ICC(1) = 0.237.

Projected-gaze rank

Correlation-matrix condition number: recorded ZuCo 304 vs projected full SST 13733. The five full-SST channels are almost one number repeated (except GD). A Linear(5→16) gaze stem cannot invent four extra degrees of freedom that are not in the table.
